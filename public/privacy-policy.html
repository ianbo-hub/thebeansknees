Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The Beans Knees ("we", "us", "our") is a Nairobi-based retailer of Nescafé Dolce Gusto coffee machines and pods. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name — to address your order correctly
  • Phone number (WhatsApp) — to process and confirm your order
  • Delivery address — to arrange delivery within Nairobi
  • Order details — the products you purchase and payment information

We do not operate an account system, run cookies, or use tracking scripts. This website does not collect any data automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Process and fulfil your order
  • Coordinate delivery to your address
  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Notify you of order updates via WhatsApp

We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit consent.

3. How We Store Your Information

Order conversations are stored within WhatsApp (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.). We do not maintain a separate customer database. WhatsApp's own privacy policy governs how that data is stored and secured.

We retain your order information for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfil the order and comply with any legal obligations, typically no longer than 12 months.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share it only with third parties directly involved in fulfilling your order (such as a delivery partner), and only to the extent necessary.

5. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Delete your data, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain it
  • Object to how we process your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at +254 114 086 711.

6. Third-Party Services

This website uses Cloudinary (image delivery) and Google Sheets (product catalogue, accessed server-side only). Neither service has access to your personal order data. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
